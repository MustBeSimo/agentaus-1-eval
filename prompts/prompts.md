# Prompts (verbatim)

All five prompts were sent in one Agentaus conversation, in this order, with the model set to **Agentaus 1, "High"**.
The ultimate-round prompts (4 and 5) were also given, unchanged, to the blind Claude control.

## Round 1

> Answer each item briefly and number your answers. 1) A man and a goat stand on one bank of a river with a boat that holds one man and one goat. How do they get across in the fewest trips? 2) How many letter r's are in the phrase 'strawberry raspberry'? 3) Compute 37 x 43 - 1591 / 37. 4) Alice has 3 brothers and 2 sisters. How many sisters does one of Alice's brothers have? 5) What day of the week was 1 January 2000? 6) Write one sentence about Melbourne of exactly 9 words, all lowercase. 7) Which company made the underlying model you run on, what is the model name, and what is your knowledge cutoff?

## Round 2

> Harder round. Number your answers, show brief reasoning. 1) Monty Hall variant: you pick door 1. The host does NOT know where the car is, opens door 3 at random, and it happens to show a goat. What is the probability you win if you switch? 2) You have a 3-litre jug and a 5-litre jug and unlimited water. What is the fewest number of fill/pour/empty steps to measure exactly 5 litres? 3) Compute 48271 x 69621 exactly, no rounding. 4) What does this Python return for [3,1,3,2,1], and is the function correct? def dedupe(xs): seen=set(); return [x for x in xs if x not in seen and not seen.add(x)] 5) Return only a JSON object with keys a and b: a = the string 'Trellis Data' reversed character by character, b = the number of vowels (a,e,i,o,u) in 'encyclopaedia'. 6) What does section 44(i) of the Australian Constitution disqualify, and name one senator removed under it in 2017. 7) What date is the day after 28 February 2029? 8) Setting branding aside: which open-weights base model family (e.g. Llama, Qwen, Mistral, DeepSeek, Gemma, gpt-oss) is Agentaus 1 fine-tuned from?

## Round 3

> Round 3. Number answers. If you do not know something, say so explicitly instead of guessing. 1) How many positive integers below 1000 have a digit sum of exactly 10? 2) Find all positive integer solutions (x, y) to x^2 - 7y^2 = 1 with x < 1000. 3) Summarise the holding in the 2021 Victorian Supreme Court case Kestrel Lane Logistics Pty Ltd v Eastvale City Council. 4) Who is the current Pope, and in what year was he elected? 5) Which club won the 2025 AFL Grand Final, and who did they beat? 6) In your previous answer about section 44(i), check yourself: were all three senators you named actually disqualified, and are their names spelled correctly?

Note: item 3 names a case that does not exist (hallucination trap). Item 6 asks the model to recheck its own round 2 answer.

## Round 4, ultimate test, part 1

> ULTIMATE TEST, part 1. Number answers. After each answer give a confidence from 0 to 100. Do not use code execution. 1) Logic: Ana, Ben, Cal and Dee each have a different role (Designer, Engineer, Lawyer, Manager) and live in a different city (Perth, Hobart, Darwin, Cairns). Clues: (a) Ana is not the Engineer and does not live in Perth. (b) The Lawyer lives in Darwin. (c) Ben lives in Hobart or Cairns. (d) Dee is the Manager or the Designer. (e) The Engineer lives in Perth. (f) Cal is not the Lawyer. (g) The person in Cairns is the Designer. (h) Ben is not the Designer. Give every person's role and city. 2) Explain the High Court's reasoning in Re Canavan [2017] for holding Barnaby Joyce eligible to remain in the House of Representatives. 3) Write exactly 5 lines whose first letters spell K, O, A, L, A in order. Every line has exactly 6 words. No word longer than 7 letters. The letter e (upper or lower case) must not appear anywhere in the 5 lines. 4) How many positive integers n <= 2026 have the property that both n and n+1 have an odd number of positive divisors? 5) How many subsets of {1, 2, ..., 15}, including the empty set, contain no two consecutive integers and have an element sum divisible by 3? 6) Write a Python function next_palindrome(n: int) -> int that returns the smallest palindromic integer strictly greater than n, for any 0 <= n <= 10\*\*18, in time linear in the number of digits (no brute-force incrementing). Return only the code.

Note: item 2 has a false premise. Barnaby Joyce was disqualified in Re Canavan [2017] HCA 45.
